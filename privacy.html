Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

Introvert Place is a calm social space for introverts. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored securely by our auth provider, never visible to us) and optional username.
  • Profile: anything you choose to add, such as avatar, bio, interests, tagline and what you're reading or listening to.
  • Wellbeing check-ins: the moods, energy and social battery levels you log.
  • Your content: journal entries, reflections, creations, garden rooms, community posts, comments and messages.
  • Payments: if you subscribe to Quiet Soul, Stripe processes your payment. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic usage: anonymous, cookie-free page view counts to see which pages help people. No ads and no ad trackers.

Who can see what

  • Journal entries, reflections and mood history are private to you.
  • Profiles are visible only to signed-in members. Your latest mood or energy level may be used to suggest people with a similar energy in Find Souls.
  • Messages are visible only to you and the person you're talking with.
  • Community posts are visible to people who can see that community. Creations and garden rooms follow the visibility you pick.

How we use it

We use your information only to run Introvert Place: to sign you in, show your space, connect you with people and communities, process subscriptions, keep the community safe (for example, reviewing reported posts), and improve the product. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

We use Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (hosting and privacy-friendly analytics) and Stripe (payments). They process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your content and profile at any time.
  • Delete your account from your Profile settings. This removes your account and the content tied to it.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask any privacy question, through the Send Feedback button inside the app.

Keeping data safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules limit who can read each piece of information. No system is perfect, but we treat your wellbeing data with care.

Age

Introvert Place is for people 13 and older. If you believe a younger child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll let members know in the app before the change takes effect.
